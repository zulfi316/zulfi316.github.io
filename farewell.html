▶ Entry: "The Traveller — Current Status" The human designated traveller has completed a circuit of several moderately remarkable locations on the third planet of an utterly insignificant little blue-green sun, far out in the uncharted backwaters of the unfashionable end of the western spiral arm of the Galaxy.

The trips, on the whole, went rather well. The dolphins were informed. They said it was fine and left, which is the traditional way these things are handled.
▶ Entry: "Planning — A Retrospective" It is an important and popular fact that things are not always what they seem. For instance, on the planet Earth, a trip planner always seems like a perfectly reasonable idea at the time of booking. It is only once you're standing in a Calgary arrivals hall at 12:20 on a Saturday that you begin to wonder whether the dolphins had the right idea all along.

The answer, of course, is 42. Or failing that, another trip.
